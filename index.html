01The essentials

What does an EU trademark protect?

Read the essentials

An EU trademark can protect a sign used to distinguish your goods or services across EU member states. The scope depends on the mark and the goods and services covered by the registration.

02Getting started

Before you apply: three things to consider

Read the essentials

Consider whether your mark is distinctive, which goods and services you need to cover, and whether earlier rights could present obstacles. An initial assessment does not guarantee registration.

03The process

Understanding EUIPO official fees

Read the essentials

Official application fees are paid to the European Union Intellectual Property Office. They are separate from professional service fees and depend on the scope of your application. Confirm current fees with EUIPO before applying.
