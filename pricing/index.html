Before you instruct us.

  1. A defined scope.

    The agreed instruction should say which mark, goods and services, territory and deliverables the work covers.

  2. Separate amounts.

    Professional fees, official fees and any other agreed costs should be identified separately, including applicable tax treatment.

  3. No unexpected next step.

    Any work outside the original instruction needs its own scope and agreement before proceeding.

Questions about cost.

Is the free assessment a full clearance search?

+

No. It is a limited preliminary look. Comprehensive clearance is a separately scoped search and analysis service. Neither a preliminary assessment nor clearance guarantees registration.

How are the costs confirmed?

+

The scope, professional fees, applicable VAT and official fees are confirmed before instruction. Additional work requires a separate agreement.

Does paying the official fee guarantee registration?

+

No. The official fee relates to the application procedure. EUIPO examines the application and earlier-rights holders may oppose. Payment does not guarantee that the mark will be registered.

When is an application filed?

+

Filing follows agreement of the service scope and fees, confirmation of the application details and your approval. A preliminary check is not an application.
