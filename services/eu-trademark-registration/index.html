    Request a free lawyer review and report to help you consider your options. A more detailed search may be appropriate.

    If you decide to proceed, we confirm the scope and fees, prepare your application and ask for your approval.

  4. 04

    File with EUIPO

    Once authorisation and fees are in place, we file your application. EUIPO then examines it and decides whether to register it.

A preliminary check and review are not comprehensive clearance or a guarantee of availability. Registration is not guaranteed.

Before you decide.

Can MARKEE help me apply for the SME Fund?

+

We can offer assistance with an SME Fund application for an additional professional fee when the programme is available. Eligibility, programme conditions, scope and fees must be confirmed; funding is not guaranteed. This optional service can be discussed if you decide to proceed with registration.

How do I get started?

+

Start with the free Trademark Check. You can then request a lawyer’s review and report before deciding whether to proceed with registration. The check is currently a demonstration and services are not yet open.

How much does registration cost?

+

The Trademark Check and lawyer review are free. MARKEE’s professional registration fee is to be confirmed, and EUIPO official fees are separate. The scope, fees and applicable taxes will be explained before you commit.

Is clearance included in registration?

+

The free check is limited and does not replace comprehensive clearance. The scope of any further clearance will be explained and confirmed before you commit.

Does an EU trademark protect me outside the EU?

+

An EU trademark covers EU member states. If you need protection in other territories, that requires separate consideration and may involve other applications.

What happens if an objection or opposition is raised?

+

The issue needs to be reviewed before deciding how to respond. This work is not automatically included in the filing service. Its scope and fees would be agreed separately.

Does a request to MARKEE file an application?

+

No. A request to MARKEE is separate from an application to the trademark office. The filing follows agreement of the professional scope, confirmation of the details and approval of the application.
