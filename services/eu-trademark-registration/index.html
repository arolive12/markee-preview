From check to application.

Start with the free check, then decide whether registration is right for you.

  1. 01

    Start with a free Trademark Check

    Tell us about your trademark and business. Preliminary findings can help identify potential obstacles; they do not establish availability.

  2. 02

    Receive your lawyer’s review

    Request a free lawyer review and report before deciding whether to proceed. A more detailed search may be appropriate.

  3. 03

    Confirm your application

    If you decide to proceed, confirm the scope and fees, provide any additional details and approve the proposed filing.

  4. 04

    We file with EUIPO

    Once the required authorisation and fees are in place, MARKEE files your application. EUIPO examines it and decides whether to register it.

A preliminary check and review are not comprehensive clearance or a guarantee of availability. Registration is not guaranteed.

Fees, clearly explained.

The Trademark Check and lawyer review are free, even if you choose not to register. EU registration is a separate professional service.
