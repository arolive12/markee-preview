Behind every brand,
someone building something.

A first idea. A carefully made product. A business finding its feet. MARKEE is being built for the people who turn those beginnings into something of their own.

Protection should start with understanding.

Trademark work can feel unfamiliar, especially when you are building a business at the same time. A useful service should make the questions easier to understand, without making the decisions seem simpler than they are.

That is the idea behind MARKEE: a digital-first approach to EU trademark protection, with clear explanations, a defined scope and appropriate professional judgement. Less time trying to understand the process. More room to make a considered decision.

The way we’re building it.

01

Explain the why.

Plain language helps you understand what is being considered, what remains uncertain and why a next step might matter.

02

Make space for judgement.

Digital tools can organise information and reduce friction. The important decisions still need context and appropriate professional review.

03

Be clear about the scope.

A preliminary check, comprehensive clearance and registration do different work. Their limits and costs should be clear from the outset.
